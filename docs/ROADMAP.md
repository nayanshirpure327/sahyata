# ROADMAP.md

## Phase 1 – MVP (4–6 weeks)
- Auth + College/City selector
- Explore page
- Stay (PG)
- Food (Tiffin)
- Jobs
- Basic Marketplace
- Notes & PYQs upload/browse

## Phase 2 – Growth
- Services (Laundry, Xerox, etc.)
- Events
- Health
- Ratings & Reviews
- Favorites
- Admin panel

## Phase 3 – Scale
- Multi-city expansion
- In-app chat
- Verified providers
- Mobile app (React Native / Expo)
- Advanced search & recommendations

## Long-term Vision
Become the default “operating system” for student life in every major Indian college city.
